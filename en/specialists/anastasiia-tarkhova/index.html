Experience and approach

Anastasiia has higher education in psychology and pedagogy and is certified in classic massage. She combines attention to a person’s emotional state with gentle bodywork, helping clients relax and reconnect with their bodies.

Areas of practice

  • Classic massage
  • Relaxation massage
  • Buccal massage
  • Trigger-point work
  • Passive exercise

Common reasons to book

  • High stress levels
  • Acute and chronic anxiety
  • Chronic headaches
  • Bruxism and tension in the jaw muscles
  • Tension in the face, neck and shoulders
  • The need for deep relaxation and recovery

Working style

She works gently and attentively, adapting the intensity to each client’s condition and needs. Her sessions combine classic and relaxation techniques with trigger-point work, attention to the jaw and facial area, and elements of passive exercise.
