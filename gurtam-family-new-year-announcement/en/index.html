Event concept

New Year traditions from around the world

Join us for a New Year celebration in the countryside. Traditions from around the world bring us together to share wishes, make memories, and start the year with rituals of our own.

Explore themed stations, creative workshops, music, and places to relax. Move between activities and enjoy the day at your own pace.

Gurtam Family New Year is a relaxed countryside celebration. It’s a chance to spend time together, make some Gurtam traditions of our own, and start the year with good memories.

Preliminary event program

January 16, 2027 · Timing and details are subject to change

  1. 1:00 p.m.Departure from the Gurtam office

    We’re planning a group transfer from the Gurtam office to Disveli Estate. We’ll share the meeting point and boarding details closer to the event.

  2. 3:00–4:00 p.m.Arrival and welcome lunch

    We’ll start with a hearty buffet lunch. There’ll be time to eat, get to know the venue, and catch up with colleagues before the afternoon program.

  3. From 4:00 p.m.Themed activities

    Write a letter to your future self, make a holiday ornament, prepare a wish for the chichilaki, relax by the fire, or drop into the Christmas Movie Lounge. Visit the stations at your own pace, with music throughout the day.

  4. 7:00 p.m.Festive dinner

    At 7:00 p.m., we’ll gather around a shared table in the Disveli Estate hall for dinner and conversation.

  5. After dinnerEvening program

    After dinner, we’ll gather to burn the chichilaki together with our New Year wishes. Then you can dance to your favorite songs, sing along to familiar hits, try different cocktails at the bar, play pool, or sit by the fire and catch up with colleagues.

  6. At the end of the eveningReturn transfer

    Two return transfers are tentatively scheduled for 10:00 p.m. and midnight.

Venue

Disveli Estate

Disveli, Bolnisi Municipality, Kvemo Kartli, Georgia

We chose Disveli Estate for its variety of spaces. The panoramic hall is ideal for lunch, dinner, and dancing, with separate areas for workshops and watching films. The venue will be exclusively ours for the celebration.
